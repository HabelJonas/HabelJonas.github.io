Privacy Policy for the App DueCue

The protection of your personal data is a top priority for us. DueCue is a subscription tracker that works entirely on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync and no backend server. We do not collect, receive, or process any of your personal data.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

The information you enter in DueCue is stored only in a local database on your device. This includes subscription names, prices, currencies, billing cycles and dates, categories, notes, price history, reminder settings and app preferences. This data is never transmitted to us or any third parties, and we have no access to it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Backups and Exports

Android cloud backup (Google Drive backup) is disabled for DueCue, so your data is not uploaded automatically. A direct device-to-device transfer, for example when setting up a new phone, may copy the app data to your new device.

You can create a backup file (JSON) or a spreadsheet report (CSV) from the settings. These files are created only when you choose to, and are saved to a location you select using the Android file picker. DueCue does not need storage permissions for this. If you save a file to a cloud storage provider, that provider's privacy policy applies to the file.

3. In-App Purchase (Premium)

DueCue Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google. We do not receive your payment details. To confirm your Premium access, even while offline, the app stores the purchase confirmation provided by Google Play (purchase token and signature) locally on your device. Communication with Google Play Billing is the only network connection the app makes. Please refer to the Google Privacy Policy for information about how Google handles purchase data.

4. Access Permissions

DueCue does not request any permissions that would allow access to your personal information (such as contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, or device identifiers). The app uses the following permissions:

  • Notifications: to remind you of upcoming subscription renewals. Reminders are scheduled and shown locally on your device. You can deny this permission or turn reminders off at any time.
  • Internet and network state: used only by Google Play Billing for the Premium purchase.
  • Run at startup, wake lock and foreground service: used by Android's background task scheduler so that reminders are still delivered after your device restarts.

5. Analytics and Advertising

We do not use any analytics tools (such as Google Analytics), crash reporting services, or advertising networks that could track your user behavior.

6. Hosting and App Stores

When downloading the app, information is transmitted to the respective app store (e.g., Google Play Store). We have no influence over this data collection and are not responsible for it. Please refer to the privacy policy of the respective store operator.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not hold any of your data, we cannot provide information, deletion, or blocking of such data. You can delete all data stored by DueCue at any time by deleting it within the app, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app. If you have any further questions, please feel free to contact us at the email address provided above.
