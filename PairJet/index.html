Privacy Policy for the App PairJet

The protection of your personal data is a top priority for us. PairJet transfers files directly between two Android devices over your local Wi-Fi network. There is no PairJet account, no transfer server, no relay and no cloud storage. We do not receive or store your files or any of your personal data on our own servers. However, PairJet uses Google services for QR scanning, advertising, privacy choices and purchases, and these services process some data as described below.

1. Your File Transfers

Files travel directly from the sending device to the receiving device you select, over an encrypted (TLS 1.3) connection on your local Wi-Fi network. The file contents, filenames, file sizes and the temporary pairing code from the QR code are sent only to that receiver. They are never sent to us, to advertising services or to purchase services. File sharing works without an internet connection.

2. QR Code Scanning

To pair two devices, the receiver shows a QR code that the sender scans with the camera. Scanning uses Google ML Kit, which processes the camera images and the decoded QR contents entirely on your device. These images and contents are not sent to Google or to us. The ML Kit library may send device and app details, installation identifiers and operational metrics (such as processing time, image configuration, events and errors) to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics when internet access is available. See the ML Kit Terms and the ML Kit data disclosure for details.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

PairJet stores only a small amount of information locally on your device: the reference to the receiving folder you chose and the access grant for it, a verified Premium purchase confirmation, and references to unfinished received files so that they can be cleaned up. PairJet does not keep a history of your transfers. This data is never transmitted to us and is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Files you receive are saved in the folder you selected. They remain there until you delete them, even if you uninstall PairJet. Google SDKs used by the app may also keep their own local preferences, identifiers, caches or queued diagnostic data.

4. Advertising

The free version of PairJet may show a single banner ad from Google AdMob on the idle home screen. We request non-personalized ads only. No ads are shown while pairing or transferring, when you have declined or not yet made a required privacy choice, or when you own Premium.

Even for non-personalized ads, Google may process your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), interactions with the app and ads, and performance diagnostics for the purposes of serving ads, measurement and fraud prevention. PairJet never provides file contents, filenames, QR codes or pairing information to advertising services. Please refer to How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your Privacy Choices

PairJet uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for and manage your privacy and advertising choices where this is required, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. When you open "Privacy & ads" in the app, the UMP contacts Google, processes request, device and app information, and saves your choices locally on your device. You can revisit your choices at any time via "Privacy options" on the home screen. Sending and receiving files always remains available, even if you decline or the service is unavailable.

6. In-App Purchase (Premium)

PairJet Premium is a one-time purchase that removes advertising. It is handled by Google Play Billing, and payment is processed entirely by Google. We do not receive your payment details. The app reads the purchase records provided by Google Play, verifies and acknowledges them on your device, and stores a small purchase confirmation locally so Premium also works offline. Restoring Premium on a new device may require internet access and the Google Play account that made the purchase. Please refer to the Google Privacy Policy for information about how Google handles purchase data.

7. Access Permissions

PairJet does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone or photo library. The app uses the following permissions:

  • Camera: to scan the receiver's QR code for pairing. Images are processed on your device only. You can deny this permission at any time.
  • Internet, network state, network changes and local network access: to connect the two devices over your local Wi-Fi network, and for the Google services described above (ads, privacy choices, purchases and scanner diagnostics).
  • Foreground service, wake lock and notifications: to keep an accepted transfer running when the screen is off or the app is in the background, and to show its progress in a notification. You can deny the notification permission at any time.

Files to send are chosen by you through the Android file picker, and received files are saved to a folder you select. PairJet does not need general storage permissions for this.

8. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We do not use any additional analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) or crash reporting services. The Google scanner and advertising libraries described above may still process operational diagnostics.

9. Hosting and App Stores

When downloading the app, information is transmitted to the respective app store (e.g., Google Play Store). We have no influence over this data collection and are not responsible for it. Please refer to the privacy policy of the respective store operator.

10. Audience

PairJet is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The app does not ask for your name, birthdate, identity or a login.

11. Data Retention and Your Rights

Since we do not hold any of your data, we cannot provide information, deletion, or blocking of such data. You can delete all data stored by PairJet at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Received files stay in your chosen folder until you delete them. Data processed by Google is subject to Google's own retention practices, and you can exercise your rights regarding that data directly with Google. If you have any further questions, please feel free to contact us at the email address provided above.
