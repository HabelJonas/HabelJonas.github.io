Privacy Policy for the App Ourli

The protection of your personal data is a top priority for us. Ourli is a household organizer that lets you share a pantry, shopping lists, tasks and a calendar with the members of your household. To sync this data between devices and members, Ourli uses an account and a server. All household content is end-to-end encrypted on your device with keys that only you and the members of your household hold. We store it only in encrypted form and cannot read it. This policy explains which data we can see and why.

1. Your Account

To use Ourli you sign in with your email address, either with a one-time code or link sent by email or with a password. Where offered, you can also sign in with your Google account; Google then shares your email address and an identity token with us. We process the following account data:

  • Email address: to sign you in, send sign-in codes, confirmation and password reset emails. Your email address is also shown to the other members of households you belong to.
  • Display name and avatar color: shown to the members of your households.
  • Password: if you choose one, it is stored only as a secure hash.
  • Household memberships and roles (owner or member) and the date you joined, to control who can access which household.

Your sign-in session is stored on your device, encrypted with a key held by the Android Keystore.

2. Household Content (End-to-End Encrypted)

Pantry items, shopping lists, tasks, calendar events and household names are encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) before they are uploaded. The household key is shared with each member individually, encrypted to that member's public key (X25519). We never receive these keys and therefore cannot decrypt your household content.

To sync the encrypted records, our server stores technical metadata that is not encrypted: record IDs and record type (e.g. pantry item, task or event), the household a record belongs to, which account created or last changed it, timestamps, the size of the encrypted record, and the key version used.

3. Encryption Keys and Recovery Key

When you set up Ourli, a key pair is created on your device. We store your public key, so other members can share household keys with you, and a copy of your private key encrypted with your recovery key, so you can restore access on a new device. The recovery key is shown to you once and is never sent to us. On your device, the private key is protected by the Android Keystore.

Because we never have your keys, we cannot recover your household content if you lose your recovery key and all of your signed-in devices.

4. Invites

Owners can invite others with a QR code, a code or a link. An invite contains a secret that is never sent to us; the server stores only the invite ID, the household's keys encrypted with that secret, who created and accepted the invite, and when it expires. If you share an invite through another app (e.g. a messenger), that app's privacy policy applies. Invites expire after 7 days by default and are deleted from our server 30 days after they expire.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

Ourli keeps an encrypted copy of your household records and pending changes on your device so it works offline. Records are decrypted only in memory while the app is in use. The app also stores your settings (such as theme, reminder times and the app lock setting) and the household you last opened. This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Signing out locks your keys on the device; deleting your account removes them and the local copy of your records. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of it.

6. Camera and Scanning

The camera is used to scan product barcodes, best-before dates and invite QR codes. Scanning uses Google ML Kit, which processes the camera images entirely on your device. Images are never uploaded to us or to Google. The ML Kit library may send device and app details and operational metrics (such as processing time, events and errors) to Google for diagnostics when internet access is available. See the ML Kit Terms and the ML Kit data disclosure for details.

7. Online Product Lookup (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a product barcode, Ourli can look up the product name, brand and quantity on Open Food Facts, a public food product database. Only the barcode number is sent; as with any internet request, Open Food Facts also receives your IP address. This lookup is enabled by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings under "Online product lookup". Please refer to the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

8. Notifications and App Lock

Reminders (morning summary, expiry alerts, task and event reminders) are created on your device from your decrypted data. They are not sent through our server or a push service, and their content is hidden on the lock screen.

If you turn on the optional app lock, Ourli asks for your fingerprint, face or device PIN using Android's biometric prompt and hides its content from screenshots and the recent apps view. Biometric data is handled by Android and never accessible to Ourli.

9. Subscription (Ourli Family)

Ourli is free for one person. To share a household with others, the owner can subscribe to Ourli Family through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google; we do not receive your payment details. The app passes a pseudonymous account identifier (a one-way hash of your user ID) and the household ID to Google Play with the purchase.

To grant access securely, our server verifies the purchase with Google Play and stores the purchase token, the subscribed product (household size), the subscription state, expiry date and auto-renewal status, linked to the household and the purchasing account. Google Play also notifies our server about renewals and cancellations. Please refer to the Google Privacy Policy for information about how Google handles purchase data.

10. Service Providers

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) hosts our authentication, database, real-time sync and server functions. It processes the account data, metadata and encrypted content described above on our behalf, as well as technical access data such as IP addresses in server logs, which are needed for operation and security. Supabase may process data outside the European Union; such transfers are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Email delivery: sign-in and account emails are sent through an email service provider on our behalf, which receives your email address and the content of these emails.
  • Google processes data for Google Play Billing, optional Google sign-in and ML Kit as described above.
  • Open Food Facts receives scanned barcodes when online product lookup is enabled.

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it for advertising.

11. Access Permissions

Ourli does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone or photo library. The app uses the following permissions:

  • Camera: to scan barcodes, best-before dates and invite QR codes. Images are processed on your device only. You can deny this permission at any time and enter items manually.
  • Notifications: to show reminders. You can deny this permission or turn reminders off at any time.
  • Internet and network state: to sign in, sync your encrypted data, verify subscriptions and, if enabled, look up products.

When you export your data, you choose where to save the file using the Android file picker. Ourli does not need storage permissions for this.

12. Analytics and Advertising

We do not use any analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), crash reporting services, or advertising networks that could track your user behavior. The Google ML Kit library described above may still process operational diagnostics.

13. Hosting and App Stores

When downloading the app, information is transmitted to the respective app store (e.g., Google Play Store). We have no influence over this data collection and are not responsible for it. Please refer to the privacy policy of the respective store operator.

14. Legal Basis

We process your account data, the encrypted household data and its metadata, and subscription data to provide the Ourli service you have signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Server logs are processed based on our legitimate interest in operating a secure and reliable service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The online product lookup and the optional Google sign-in are used only if you choose them.

15. Audience

Ourli can be used by families. Accounts should be created by, or with the consent of, a parent or guardian where required by law.

16. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your account data is stored for as long as you have an account.
  • Items you delete are removed from our server within 60 days, after all devices have had time to sync the deletion.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account & data. This deletes your account, profile and keys. Households you own are transferred to the longest-standing other member, or deleted together with all their data if you are the only member. Content you created in shared households remains available to the other members, without being linked to your account. You can also request deletion without the app; see Delete your Ourli account.
  • Subscription records are kept as long as required for billing and legal obligations; after account deletion they are no longer linked to your account. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; please cancel it in Google Play.

17. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, delete and export your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. You can exercise many of these rights directly in the app: change your display name, export a decrypted copy of your household data in Settings → Export household data, and delete your account. Since we cannot read your household content, we can only provide it in encrypted form. For anything else, please contact us at the email address provided above.

18. Changes

We may update this privacy policy when the app changes. We will announce material changes in the app.
